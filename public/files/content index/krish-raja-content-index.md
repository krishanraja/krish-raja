1. Match content to images using `appearance_id`. Do not infer a match from a similar title alone.
2. Use an image only when `screenshot_status` is `approved`.
3. Never publish images marked `reject` or `unavailable`. Those records exist to make failures explicit.

5. `capture_source` is the URL actually shown in the screenshot. Other URLs are alternate distributions or supporting evidence.
